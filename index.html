About PAF-IAST

Message from Rector

Being one of the pioneer members of the project team, I must say that inception of Pak-Austria Fachhochschule Institute of Applied Sciences and Technology is like a dream come true. I take immense pride in being appointed as the first Rector of the Institute.

Today, knowledge along with skill is the key difference between developed and underdeveloped economies. Pakistan lacks in adequately trained and skilled work force in production sector, keeping it behind others. To become a developed economy, it is imperative for the nation to invest in and leverage its engineering sector.

Our Green Campus

PAF IAST is nestled in natural mountains that flank its campus from all sides, offering one even the ravishing view of snow-capped peaks of Nathiagali and natural winding trails. Set in the middle of the campus is a natural lake, fed by the springs of surrounding mountains.

The ideal setting equally conducive to academic activity and aesthetic sensibilities of the human eye is a serene island of its own kind, far from the chaos, congestion and cacophony of metropolitan culture.

Skilling Pakistan

The project concept stems from the slogan “Skilling Pakistan” and envisages creating highly credible technical education infrastructure both at tertiary and hi-tech industry levels.

We aim to raise the standards of technical education in Pakistan at par with those of most advanced industrial economies. We are fully conscious of the need to link employment with education by harnessing high-quality manpower.

Centers of Excellence

PAF IAST boasts the latest practical laboratories equipped at par with partner universities to keep pace with the highest standards of learning outcomes.

Sino-Pak Center for Artificial Intelligence

The core objective of the center is to build national capacity for R&D in the emerging field of artificial intelligence and gain expertise in this area through MS/Ph.D. programs in collaboration with Chinese and Austrian universities. Besides providing high-value services to industrial partners, we seek to achieve the capability to solve local problems using AI and market those indigenous solutions through the technology park.

Read More

Mineral Resource Engineering

Pakistan has rich reservoirs of minerals resources, including clays, copper, dolomite, gypsum, iron ore, limestone, marble (onyx), salt, sand and gravel, silica sand, as well as energy resources like coal, natural gas, oil; and precious and novel stones. Considering the significance of mineral resources, PAF IAST has set up a Centre of excellence with the co-operation of Chinese universities.

Agriculture Food Technologies

Agriculture has remained the lifeline of Pakistan’s economy since its birth. It significantly contributes to the national economy and poverty alleviation by providing food security to the masses, besides supplying cheap raw material to several value-added industries. PAF IAST plans to establish a center of excellence in Agriculture Food Technology with the help of Chinese universities in critical areas such as Food Processing Technology.

Read More

Transportation / Railway Engineering

The Center of Excellence aims to become a world-class educational institution for imparting knowledge, skill development and research in the field of transport and railways. Its core domain will be to impart high-quality education initially in rail-related matters; provide training opportunities; create an environment of excellence in focused rail and transport sector R&D; and liaise with the transport and rail industry to provide solutions to tackle emerging technical challenges.

Campus Life

Experience a thriving, supportive, and advanced academic environment.

Academic Blocks

Well-ventilated smart classrooms, state-of-the-art labs, ubiquitous Wi-Fi, and spacious open areas reserved for students.

Central Library

High standard library provided with course textbooks, reference books, and smart cubicles for discussions.

Hostels

Fully furnished shared accommodation for resident students including dining halls, indoor games, and laundry.

Sports Facilities

Large grounds reserved for cricket and soccer, jogging tracks, plus badminton and volleyball courts.

Student Activity Center

Custom-built for recreation featuring a state-of-the-art cafeteria, stationery shop, and printing facilities.

Auditoriums

Independent auditoriums with 100-person capacities, equipped with internet connectivity and lecture recording provisions.

Biometric & RFID System

Advanced RFID systems installed to monitor attendance and secure movement across the campus buildings.

On-Campus School

A separate building for kindergarten to intermediate level, along with a daycare facility for faculty and administrative officials.
